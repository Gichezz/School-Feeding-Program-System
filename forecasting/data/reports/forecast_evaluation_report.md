Rolling-origin evaluation: a forecast origin every 14 calendar days (45 validation origins, 45 test origins). At each origin, models use only school days before the origin (expanding history) and forecast every school day in the next 30 calendar days that falls in the same split. Horizons 7, 14, 30 score the targets up to 7, 14 or 30 days ahead. Prophet is refitted at every origin. Selection uses validation only; the test period is evaluated once.
