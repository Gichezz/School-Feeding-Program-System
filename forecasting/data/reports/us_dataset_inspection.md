## 9. Relevance to attendance and meal-demand forecasting

| Field | Content | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| School DBN | School identifier | Grouping only; US-specific code, not reused |
| Date | Calendar date of a school day | Used to derive day-of-week and break-adjacency patterns |
| Enrolled | Students on register | Used only to compute rates; US enrolment levels are not reused |
| Present | Students present | Attendance-rate reference (via Present / Enrolled) |
| Absent | Students absent | Redundant with Present (identity above) |
| Released | Undocumented count | Not used |

The dataset contains **no** meal, weather, holiday, examination or special-event fields. It only covers school days, so non-school days are absent rather than recorded.

## 10. Data-quality issues

- Only one school year (185 dates) - too short to learn yearly seasonality directly.
- 7 dates have very few schools reporting; including them would distort aggregates.
- 3 schools have fewer than 100 records (openings, closures or reporting gaps).
- 958 rows have Released >= half of Enrolled, so Present+Absent can be far below Enrolled.
- 3,161 school-days have an attendance rate below 0.5.
- 43 rows have fewer than 20 enrolled students (rates are very noisy).
- Date stored as an integer, not an ISO date.
- No documentation of field definitions inside the file.
